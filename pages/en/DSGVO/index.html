2. What data is collected?

Locally stored data

MyWorkLog speichert folgende Informationen primarily local im Local Storage Ihres Browsers:

  • Time-tracking data (start and end times, break times)
  • Entry types and categories (work, school, vacation, sick, holiday)
  • Grades and evaluations
  • Personal settings and preferences
  • Goals and progress
  • Feedback history and feature requests

Optional cloud synchronization (Supabase)

If you enable cloud synchronization and log in, your locally stored data is additionally stored on servers of Supabase (Supabase Inc., USA) gespeichert. Details hierzu finden Sie in Abschnitt 4.

Web analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics)

On this website, aggregated, anonymized page views are collected via Cloudflare Web Analytics collected. This product is cookieless: It sets no cookies, does no fingerprinting, does not permanently store IP addresses and performs no cross-site tracking. Only aggregated metrics such as page views, referrer, browser type and country are processed. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) lit. f GDPR (legitimate interest in statistical reach measurement).

Product analytics (PostHog, EU hosting)

In addition, PostHog is used to understand how the application is used and to improve its features. Processing takes place exclusively on the PostHog EU servers (eu.i.posthog.com, hosted in Frankfurt). No transfer to the USA takes place.

What is collected:

  • Page views and page exits — which page was opened and left, and when
  • Technical characteristics — browser, operating system, device type, screen and window size, language setting
  • Approximate location — country, region and city, derived from the IP address. The IP address itself is not stored.
  • Load performance (Web Vitals) — technical measurements such as the load time of the largest visible element (LCP). Pure timing data, no personal reference.
  • Feature usage — what is counted is that an action took place (e.g. "entry created", "timer used", "data exported"), together with a coarse category such as the entry type. No content is transmitted — no working hours, no notes, no projects, no names, no amounts. Your entries stay in your browser only.

PostHog is configured so that no person profiles are created (person_profiles: 'never') and no automatic click tracking stattfindet (autocapture: false) takes place. Only the explicitly defined events listed above are recorded.

To distinguish returning sessions, PostHog stores a randomly generated identifier in the browser (local storage or a first-party cookie). This identifier contains no personal data and is not used across websites.

The analysis produced from this data is publicly visible: Analytics dashboard. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in improving and statistically evaluating the application). Objection: Collection can be prevented with a browser ad blocker or a "Do Not Track" setting; the application works unchanged either way. Details: PostHog Privacy Policy.

Hosting & server logs (Cloudflare Pages)

This application is hosted on Cloudflare Pages (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When the page is accessed, Cloudflare technically necessarily processes the following data:

  • IP address (for delivering content and for DDoS/bot protection)
  • Date and time of access
  • Requested URL, HTTP status code, amount of data transferred
  • Referrer and user agent

This data is processed in server logs and aggregated or deleted after a short time. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) lit. f GDPR (legitimate interest in stable, secure operation of the application). Third-country transfer: Cloudflare is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework; in addition, standard contractual clauses (SCC) are in place. Details: Cloudflare Privacy Policy and Cloudflare GDPR.

Own tracking — there is none

MyWorkLog setzt no own visitor tracking. In particular, this page contains no:

  • Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager or similar analytics pixels
  • Meta/Facebook pixel, TikTok pixel or comparable advertising trackers
  • Profiling or automated decision-making within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR
  • Cross-site tracking or sale of data to third parties

Note: For the Google Search Console the domain is verified as property. The Search Console does not process any visitor data of this website; it merely provides the operator with statistics about Google search results. No personal data about you is collected through this.

Feedback function (optional)

When voluntarily sending feedback in "Full" mode, device information (screen size, platform, language, user agent) is sent along. This only happens after your explicit consent — see section 4 for details.

Data security: By default, all content data stays on your device. You have full control over when and how this data is deleted. Transmission to third parties only takes place with your explicit consent or insofar as strictly necessary for technical operation (hosting).

3. Data storage and security

Where is your data stored?

By default, your data is stored in your browser's local storage . Optionally, you can enable cloud synchronization:

  • Local (default): All data remains exclusively on your device
  • Cloud (optional): When synchronization is enabled, data is additionally stored encrypted at Supabase (see section 4)

Security measures

  • Browser security: Your browser protects local storage through its security model (same-origin policy)
  • Local control: Only you have access to your locally stored data
  • Encryption: Any data transmission (cloud sync, feedback) takes place via HTTPS/TLS
  • Backup: You can export your data as a JSON file at any time
  • Deletion: You can delete all data at any time (app settings or browser cache)

5. Ihre Rechte (DSGVO)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the following rights:

Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR)

You can request access to your stored data at any time. In MyWorkLog you can do this directly in the application by:

  • Opening the settings
  • Exporting your data
  • Viewing the JSON file

Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)

You can correct and update your data in the application at any time.

Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)

You can delete your data as follows:

  • Delete individual entries directly in the app
  • Reset all data via the settings
  • Clear browser cache and local storage

Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)

You can export a backup of your data as a JSON file at any time and import it into another application.

Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)

You can restrict processing by disabling optional features (cloud sync, feedback) or by not using the application.

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR)

You have the right to object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to the processing of your personal data.

Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR)

Without prejudice to any other legal remedy, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority if you believe that the processing of your data infringes the GDPR.

6. Cookies, Local Storage & vergleichbare Technologien

What MyWorkLog itself stores

MyWorkLog setzt no cookies itself. The application stores the data necessary for its function exclusively locally in your browser via the Web Storage APIs:

  • Local Storage: Persistent storage of your time entries, settings and preferences.
  • Session Storage: Temporary storage for the current session (deleted when the tab is closed).
  • Service Worker / Cache Storage: Offline capability and faster loading (PWA).
  • IndexedDB: Currently not used.

These mechanisms are, for the operation of the application, strictly necessary within the meaning of § 25 (2) No. 2 TDDDG and do not require consent. You can delete this data at any time via the browser settings or in the app itself.

Cookies set by our hosting provider (Cloudflare)

Since this application is delivered via the infrastructure of Cloudflare, Inc. (see section 2 — hosting), Cloudflare may set cookies in your browser as part of its bot and DDoS protection. These cookies are not controlled by MyWorkLog and cannot technically be switched off without deactivating the platform's security functions. Depending on the risk assessment of the request, they do not always occur.

Cloudflare may set the following cookies:

  • __cf_bm (Cloudflare Bot Management) — Distinguishes human from automated requests, protects against bots and scraping. Duration: approx. 30 minutes from last activity. Provider: Cloudflare, Inc.
  • cf_clearance — Only set when Cloudflare has successfully completed a security check (challenge). Duration: up to 30 days. Provider: Cloudflare, Inc.
  • _cfuvid — Complements __cf_bm in identifying legitimate requests. Duration: Session (deleted when the browser is closed). Provider: Cloudflare, Inc.

Legal basis: § 25 (2) No. 2 TDDDG (strictly necessary for the telemedia service expressly requested by the user) in conjunction with Art. 6 (1) lit. f GDPR (legitimate interest in the security and availability of the website). These cookies are classified as technically necessary for security and therefore do not require prior consent in the sense of a cookie banner.

Further information about these cookies and their processing can be found in the Cloudflare privacy policy as well as at Cloudflare Cookies (Developer Docs).

Third-party cookies from features you actively use

If you actively use optional features such as cloud sync (Supabase), the feedback function (EmailJS) or donations (PayPal), these services may set their own cookies. The respective processing is described in section 4 and only takes place with your explicit consent.

Disabling cookies in your browser

You can manage or delete cookies and local-storage data at any time in your browser settings. Please note that disabling the Cloudflare security cookies may cause the application to classify you as suspicious traffic and block access.

No marketing tracking: No Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Meta pixel or comparable advertising tracking is used. Therefore there are no corresponding cookies and no cookie banner with advertising options either.
